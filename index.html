Where do repeat offenders operate from?

A geographic profiling tool that maps crime locations by precinct and compares two estimates of where a crime series is centred: the simple mean and the density-weighted center.

How to use it

  1. Download a sample file, or filter and export a full dataset.
  2. Launch the tool and upload the file.
  3. Pick a precinct to see its cases on the map.
  4. Compare the simple mean with the density-weighted center, and check how far apart they land.

An educational project, not an operational investigative system. Results are estimates and must never be treated as evidence or used to identify real individuals. Data comes from public NYPD records via NYC Open Data.
